Pick faces straight in the 3D view, a Settings page with your printer's size, and cleaner geometry.

- **Tap a face to select it:** it lights up, and Extrude or Sketch in the toolbar then works on that face. **Hold a face** for a menu: Extrude, Cut into the model, or Sketch on this face.
- **Shapes grow in whole millimeters while you draw:** circles, arcs and polygons by diameter, lines and slots by length, rectangles by width and height. Snapping to points and edges still wins, and the grid button turns it off.
- **Settings (☰ → Settings):** language, theme and About moved here, which leaves more room for the feature list. New: **your printer's build volume** – Bambu Lab A1 mini by default, other presets or a custom size. The plate is drawn under the model, and the status bar warns when the model doesn't fit.
- **Everything renders by itself:** Custom code renders when you pause typing, so the Render button is gone. It only shows up as Cancel during a long render.
- **No more lines across flat faces** after extruding in several steps.
- **A face you extrude lines up exactly** with the face it came from – no tiny step where the new part joins.
- The app follows the phone's language for its few system messages (English or Swedish).
