Text follows round walls, goes where you tap, and moves with a finger.

- **Text on a cylinder wall:** text sketched on a round side used to be cut as a flat slab that only touched the wall along one line, so the ends of the text came out shallow or missing. It now wraps around the wall, the same depth all the way – raised or engraved, outside a post or inside a hole. Existing models get it without changes.
- **Text on a face:** with a model, **Text** asks for a face and puts the text where you tap. A selected face + **Text** works too, and holding a face offers **Text on this face**.
- **Drag to move:** with the text panel open, drag the text to slide it along its face (around the wall on a cylinder); dragging beside it still turns the view. Tap elsewhere to move it there, onto another face too. **Cancel** puts an edited text back where it was.
