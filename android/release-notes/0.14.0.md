A container is now built from ordinary parts, so text and everything else works on it.

- **Container as parts:** **OK** in the container panel no longer adds one "Container" to the tree. The box goes in as its parts: **Box** and **Cavity** extrudes, **Dividers** if you chose compartments, and the lid beside it on its own sketch (**Lid**, **Lid lip**, **Snap bead**, with a **Snap groove** in the box). A screw lid becomes **Thread** and **Lid thread** features, a **Neck** and a **Grip** repeated around the lid. Each part can be changed, suppressed or extended on its own.
- **Text on a container:** text, holes and sketches find the box's faces like on any other body. Text on a jar's wall wraps around it.
- **Shared sizes as variables:** height, wall, floor, clearance and the like become variables, so changing the height moves the cavity, the snap groove and the dividers with it. The dividers and where the lid lies are fixed sizes: if you make the box bigger, move or redraw them.
- **Older models:** a container in an older model still builds as before. Open it and tap **OK** to turn it into parts.
- **Extrude:** new **Start** (where the extrude begins, along the normal) and **Profile offset** (grow or shrink the profile, e.g. for a box's inside) fields.
