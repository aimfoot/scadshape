A new check finds cuts that remove nothing, and the code gets more room.

- **Cuts that remove nothing:** a part taken away with `difference()` that doesn't touch the shape it is cut from – outside the part, or in an opening such as the hole of a ring – renders without a word, so the engraving or hole is simply missing. The check now finds it a moment after the model renders and adds a row on the cut's line, which **Copy for AI** passes on. A typical AI mistake: text "engraved" on a ring but placed just outside its surface.
