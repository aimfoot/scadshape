The span is measured the short way across, as the slicer lays the strands. If the hollow under it is walled all round and open to the bed, the row asks whether the part is upside down or the cavity is cut through the floor – a common AI mistake.
- **Copy for AI keeps the model's purpose:** the text now asks the AI to fix the cause, and not to remove or fake a feature (such as a thread) just to make a warning go away.
- **Convert to sketches with BOSL2:** in code that includes BOSL2, every part after the first used to become an OpenSCAD code block. Now they become sketches and extrudes like the first.
