First public release of scadshape for Android: parametric CAD on your phone for 3D printing.

- Sketch with your finger, add constraints and dimensions (FreeCAD's solver), and build features: extrude, revolve, holes and threads, containers with lids, text, snap fits, patterns and fillets.
- OpenSCAD (WebAssembly, Manifold backend) renders everything on the device. **No permissions at all** – the app works fully offline and never touches the network.
- Export 3MF/STL for your slicer, or share straight to another app. Open STL, SVG and `.scad` files.
- New to CAD? The start card has four example models whose sizes you change under Variables.
- Swedish and English.

**Install:** download `scadshape-0.3.1.apk` below, or add `https://github.com/aimfoot/scadshape` in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium) to get updates. Android 10 or newer.

This is an early version – feedback and bug reports are welcome in the issues.
